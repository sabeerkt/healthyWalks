Our Services

Experience Excellence with Health & Walk

As the leading wholesale meat supplier in the UAE, we specialize in import, export, trading, storage, and mobilization of premium frozen meat. Our commitment to quality, compliance, and timely delivery ensures trust and satisfaction for all our clients.

Import

We meticulously source and import a wide range of premium frozen meat products from trusted suppliers worldwide, ensuring the highest quality for our customers.

Export

Leveraging our market expertise, we facilitate the export of premium meat products globally, maintaining excellence and compliance in every shipment.

Trading

Through strategic partnerships and market presence, we engage in trading activities that meet the evolving demands of UAE businesses and restaurants.

Storage

Our state-of-the-art cold storage facilities in Al Quoz, Rashidiyah, and DIP ensure that products remain fresh, maintaining top quality and safety standards.

Mobilization

Our own fleet of trucks guarantees prompt, reliable delivery, ensuring your orders are handled efficiently and reach you in perfect condition.
